Dataset:-https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1akV6po4Nrgkc3yQrJkzA6cJlV-wBvUYs?usp=sharing
Domain: Healthcare - Early Diabetes Detection using AI
Overview
This project is an AI solution design for early diabetes detection. The solution uses a Feed-Forward Neural Network to predict a patient's diabetes risk score based on 11 health features, enabling doctors to intervene early and prevent complications.
Problem Summary
Domain: Healthcare
Problem: Diabetes is detected too late, causing blindness, kidney failure, and amputations
AI Task: Binary Classification
Model: Feed-Forward Neural Network
Input: 11 patient health features like BMI, glucose, HbA1c, and age
Output: Risk score from 0 to 100 percent, categorized as High, Medium, or Low risk
Expected Business Impact
40 percent reduction in late-stage diabetes diagnoses
30 percent reduction in treatment costs per patient
Screening time reduced from 3 days to under 1 minute
Scalable to millions of patients across hospital networks
Responsible AI
All predictions are reviewed by qualified doctors before communicating to patients. Results are explainable using SHAP feature importance scores. The system is audited monthly for bias across demographic groups and complies with India's DPDP Act and international HIPAA standards.
